# Light Editor Dialog Box

HVE's Light Object Tool, in the 3-D Editor, creates and edits light sources placed in the environment: point, directional and spot lights with a position, a direction, a color, an intensity and, for a spot light, a cone and drop-off rate.

!!! warning "Not in HVE Studio"
    HVE Studio has no 3-D Editor, so there is no Light Editor. Lights placed in the environment are made in the Environment panel's **Lights** section instead (below).

The lights HVE Studio does have:

- **The sun and the ambient light.** **View › Shader Editor**, *Sun & Sky* section: the sun set by hand (**Sun Rotation**, **Sun Elevation**) or computed from the site's location, date and time, and its strength (**Sun Light**). The light the sky fills the scene with is the world's **Sky Texture** node's **Ambient** and **Night Light** fields. The Environment panel's **Sky** section summarizes them and has an **Edit the sun and sky...** button. **View › Scene Display › Rendering › Light Sources On** switches the sun's contribution off and on.
- **Vehicle lights.** **Vehicle › Editors › Vehicle Lights...** in the Vehicle Editor edits the lamps fixed to the selected vehicle on two tabs. **Lamp parts** lists the vehicle's lamps, one row each (**Show every part** lists the mesh's parts that are not lamps too), each with its **Lamp** function, **Side** (Left, Right or Center, or **Auto**, which shows the side it found), **State** (Auto, On or Off), **Casts** (whether it lights the road, not only glows), **Color** and **Reset**; **Lamp** and **Side** can both be changed in the list, and a row set here is kept in the case by its name (and with the vehicle by **Save to my vehicles**). A vehicle whose body file tags its lamps the way HVE does (`HVE_LIGHT_` tags, as most vehicles in the EDC database do) comes already classified: each tagged lens is its own row, named after its tag (for example `HVE_LIGHT_BRAKELIGHT_REAR_LEFT`), its **Lamp** read from the tag's type and the other tags on the same lens (a running and brake lens is a **Tail and brake lamp**, a signal and hazard lens a **Turn signal**, a rear running lamp a **Tail / running lamp** and a front one a **Side marker**), and its **Side** from the tag's description (`FrontLeft1`, `REAR_RIGHT`, `RearMiddle1`); a tag that names no side takes the side its lens sits on. In such a file the tags alone say which lenses are lamps, so its untagged lens materials are not listed as lamps (reflectors aside). A body file that tags no lamp has a row per part, and a lamp part on both sides of the car, or at both ends, is a row per place: the Acura Legend's single `LIGHTRED+2` part, a tail panel across the back, is **LIGHTRED+2 (left)** and **LIGHTRED+2 (right)**. A narrow lens across the centerline stays one **Center** row. Until you set one, such a part's **Lamp** is guessed from its name: HVE's lens materials `LIGHTWHITE` (with or without an underscore, and with an exporter's `+3` or `_001`) are a headlamp at the front and a reverse lamp at the back, `LIGHTRED` is a **Tail and brake lamp** at the back (lit after dark, and lit while the run brakes) and a side marker at the front, and `LIGHTAMBER` a turn signal; a row's front or back is where its middle sits on the body. A row's **Side** decides which beam a lamp that casts light feeds: a Left or Right lamp lights that side's beam, a Center lamp each side by where its triangles are. A row set on a whole part before it was split applies to each of its sides until a side is set on its own. **Light sources** lists the vehicle's light sources, each with a **Name**, **Enabled** switch, under **Type & Intensity** its **Type** (Point or Spot; HVE's Directional draws exactly as a spot, so a light stored Directional keeps it until changed) and **Intensity**, **Color**, **Position** (X, Y, Z on the vehicle), **Direction** as **Azimuth (deg)** and **Zenith (deg)**, and for a spot light its **Cone Angle (deg)** and **Drop-Off**. Lights are added and removed with **Add** and **Delete**. Unlike a site light, a vehicle light moves with its vehicle.
- **Site lights** — HVE's environment lights. In the Environment Editor (F4), the Environment panel's **Lights** section: **Add Light**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**, and for the selected light its **Name**, **On**, **On in daylight too**, **Type** (Point or Spot — there is no directional site light; the sun is the directional light), position as **X (forward)**, **Y (right)** and **Height above the ground**, for a spot its **Aim**, **Tilt down**, **Cone (deg, half-angle)** and **Edge softness**, and **Color** and **Intensity**. They light the 3-D view, the ray-traced render and the glTF export, and no engine reads them. See [Environment Information](../08-environment/EnvtInfoDlg.md#lights).
