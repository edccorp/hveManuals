# New Ellipsoid Dialog

In HVE this dialog asks for the name of a new contact ellipsoid when **Add** is
pressed in a human's Contact Ellipsoids dialog. The name must be unique on the
segment and at most 30 characters, and a segment may have at most 3 ellipsoids.

!!! note "HVE Studio"
    There is no separate dialog. In the **Ellipsoids** tab of the **Body
    Segments** window (Human editor, Occupants panel, **Body Segments...**),
    **Add** creates the ellipsoid at once, named after the segment and its
    number (for example "Head 2"), and **Name** renames it. **Add** is disabled
    when the segment already has 3 ellipsoids. Names are not checked for
    uniqueness. See [Human Contact Ellipsoids](../07-humans/Human1.md).
